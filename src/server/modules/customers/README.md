# Customers

Owns customer identity, profile, normalized contact fields, credential hash, verification/consent state, status, and privacy operations. It does not own addresses, carts, orders, transactions, or sessions; those modules refer to customers through opaque identifiers and public contracts.

SK-0049 policy: a mobile number is required; email is optional. Portuguese nine-digit mobile numbers beginning with `9` normalize to `+351…`; international `+` and `00` forms normalize to E.164, and Persian/Arabic numerals are accepted in input. Names allow Unicode letters and marks (including Persian and Portuguese spelling), plus ordinary internal separators. Email is trimmed, NFKC-normalized, and case-folded; blank email becomes `null`. Mobile is globally unique, and present email has a partial unique index. Both identities remain reserved when an account is disabled.

The private `customers` model stores Argon2id password hash, `mobileVerifiedAt`/`emailVerifiedAt`, positive integer `passwordVersion`, terms-acceptance timestamp, marketing-consent Boolean and grant timestamp, `active`/`disabled` status, and shared base-schema timestamps/actor metadata. Email verification requires an email; granted marketing consent requires a timestamp. Hash and password version are excluded from JSON, and the hash is excluded from ordinary queries. New passwords share the server-only [OWASP-baseline](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html) hasher with Admins. Authentication, verification delivery, consent audit events, password-version increments, and privacy workflows remain future tasks. Generic update operators must not bypass document validation.

SK-0053 exposes `registerCustomer` through this module's public API. Signup requires both terms acceptance and an explicit optional marketing choice; the server, never the browser, timestamps them. A mobile number is required, email remains optional, and both verification timestamps stay null until a delivery provider and verification flow are approved. Passwords must be at least 15 Unicode characters (and at most 1,024 UTF-8 bytes), with common trivial values rejected; no arbitrary symbol/uppercase composition rule is imposed. Concurrent duplicate mobile/email inserts are handled by unique indexes and never overwrite an existing account. Publish the final terms/privacy text and record a version before production launch; the current timestamp alone is not a complete legal audit trail.
