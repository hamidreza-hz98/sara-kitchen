The expanded scan also found a 2.94:1 small coral “MANAGEMENT” label in the dashboard sidebar; it now uses the accessible theme text shade. The menu banner had a more severe dark-mode failure: white text on fixed pale pink measured 1.06:1. Menu, About, Contact, and blog banners now switch to dark surfaces or gradients in dark mode. These fixes are covered by the dashboard-preview and dark public-page contrast checks.

Verification: six focused Playwright checks passed across light/dark theme showcase, Persian hero, four light public pages, five commerce/admin entry pages, and five dark public pages. Nine theme/token unit checks and TypeScript typecheck passed. Automated contrast scans supplement, but do not replace, review of image overlays and non-color status cues.

No brand-color exception or owner-approved low-contrast rationale is requested: these are remediations, not accepted failures. Uploaded imagery remains editorial content, but the opaque text backings make text legible independently of the image colors.
