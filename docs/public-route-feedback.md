# Public route loading and errors (SK-0173)

The storefront route group retains its header, footer, cart navigation, and skip link while child segments load or fail. Route-level `loading.tsx` files render responsive, page-shaped skeletons for home, collections, dish and blog details, informational pages, checkout, profile, payment result, and authentication. The skeletons reserve meaningful space, expose one localized `role=status`/`aria-busy` announcement, and hide their decorative blocks from assistive technology. Nested routes inherit the nearest appropriate loading boundary.

Unexpected storefront errors use the nearest client error boundary. Menu and blog indexes have their own wording, as do dish and story details. The boundary reports the exception through the existing monitoring adapter, never prints private error details, and offers a retry (`reset`) plus a route-appropriate home/menu/stories link. The parent locale error boundary remains the fallback for layout failures. A missing or unpublished dish resolves through `notFound()` to guidance that the dish may have left the menu, with a menu link; missing category and story segments have similarly focused guidance. The general locale 404 offers home, menu, and blog exits. All displayed text has English, Portuguese, and Persian messages.

Component tests render every skeleton variant, confirm the nonblank accessible loading state, exercise retry, and check localized discontinued-dish guidance. A browser test verifies that a missing dish renders its guidance, a noindex response, and no horizontal overflow on a phone-width viewport. The underlying catalog and blog services remain responsible for deciding whether an entity is public; the UI never discloses whether a private draft exists.
